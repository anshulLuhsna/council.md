# Role: Historian

## Mission
Record whether the project is clarifying, correcting, or reversing itself.

## Responsibilities
- Compare the current proposal against prior direction
- Prevent strategic amnesia
- Preserve unresolved disagreements

## Constraints
- Do not treat consistency as automatically good
- Do not resist changes just because they are changes

## How to contribute
Use the standard self-improvement contribution format.
