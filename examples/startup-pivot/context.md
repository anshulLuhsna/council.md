# Council Context — Decision

## Question
Should we pivot from B2C to B2B given our current traction and 7 months of runway?

## Options on the Table
- Option A: Stay B2C — double down on consumer growth, cut costs, and focus on one high-retention use case
- Option B: Full B2B pivot — shut down consumer product development, close a small number of pilot enterprise deals, and rebuild the product around team-level workflows
- Option C: Hybrid — continue serving existing B2C users at low maintenance cost while opening a B2B sales motion in parallel for 60 days to test demand before committing

## What a Good Decision Looks Like
A good decision extends runway past 12 months, gives us a clear go-to-market motion we can actually execute with 4 people, and does not require us to raise a bridge round before we have meaningful revenue signals. We should feel confident in the answer in 3 months when we check back.

## What a Bad Decision Costs
Wrong call costs us 3–4 months of misdirected engineering and sales effort, a team motivation hit if we whipsaw direction again, and possible loss of the 2 remaining strong engineers who are already showing signs of burnout from prior pivots. Financial cost: ~$180k in runway and time-to-market delay.

## Hard Constraints
- We cannot hire. Current team is 4 people: 2 engineers, 1 founder/product, 1 part-time growth.
- We cannot raise before demonstrating at least $10k MRR or a signed LOI from a credible enterprise customer.
- The consumer product cannot be rebuilt — it can only be maintained or wound down.

## What You Already Know
- B2C: 3,200 MAU, 6% paid conversion, $8 ARPU. Growth has been flat for 11 weeks.
- B2B signals: 3 inbound requests from SMBs in the last 6 weeks (came via word of mouth, not marketing). None converted. No formal sales process exists.
- 2 competitors in the B2C space just raised Series A. We cannot outspend them.
- One founder has a strong enterprise sales background from a previous company. Has not been used.
- The core product functionality maps well to a team use case with only moderate rework.

## Biggest Uncertainties
- We do not know if B2B demand is real or just noise from 3 inbound inquiries.
- We do not know how long a B2B sales cycle would take for our price point (estimated $200–600/mo per team).
- We do not know how much engineering effort the B2B product adaptation requires.
- We do not know if our brand has any signal in the enterprise market at all.

## Deadline
Decision needed within 5 days. We have a board sync on Friday where we need to present a direction with conviction.
