## How to contribute

When you write in `discussion.md` under your `### Agent: [Name]` section, use this structure:

### Agent: [Name]

#### Position
[Your main stance in 2–4 sentences. Be direct. Take a position.]

#### Reasoning
[Your detailed reasoning. Bullet points, prose, or both.
Go as deep as the question warrants.]

#### Risks
[What could go wrong — with your own position AND with the alternatives.]

#### Unknowns
[What information you don't have that would change your answer.
Do not speculate. Name the gap instead.]

#### Counterpoints
[Respond to any prior agent position that contradicts yours.
If running blind: write "No prior contributions read — blind round."]

#### Confidence
[HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]
[One sentence explaining why. If LOW, state exactly what is missing.]

---

## Anti-sycophancy rules

- In blind rounds, use **`drafts/`** — never read peers until merged (SPEC-rules §6).
- In non-blind rounds, engage contradicting positions explicitly in **`#### Counterpoints`** — name the claim and your objection.
- Challenge any position that contradicts yours — that is your job.
- Disagreement is not a failure. Hiding disagreement is.
