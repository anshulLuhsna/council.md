## Starting Point
[Where are you now? What exists, what has been done, and what is the current situation?]

## Planning Horizon
[Over what timeframe does this plan span? What milestones mark progress?]

## Constraints
[What limits the plan? Time, budget, team capacity, technology, hard deadlines, dependencies.]

## What Is Already Committed
[What decisions have already been made that the plan must work around?]

## Biggest Uncertainties
[What about the path forward is most unclear or risky?

## What a Good Plan Looks Like
[How will you recognize a good plan? What qualities must it have?
Practical? Sequenced correctly? Resilient? Achievable with current resources?]
